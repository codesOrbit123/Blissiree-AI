# Content Safety Review

Imported titles are automatically flagged—not deleted—when they reference self-harm, suicide, trauma, medical/body conditions, pain, addiction, eating disorders, pregnancy, children, or treatment/healing claims.

Current automated queue:

- 56 titles require safety review.
- 42 titles require claim review.

Automated flags are conservative screening aids, not approval decisions. Blissiree remains non-medical and stores no AI diagnosis or medical record.

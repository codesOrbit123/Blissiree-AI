# Boost Content Model

Boosts are short-term support content and are never interchangeable with Programs. The canonical machine-readable catalog is `knowledge/catalog/content.json`.

Every Boost preserves its exact source title and includes review, eligibility, provenance, collection membership, audience, age, sleep, safety, and approved-use fields. Unknown fields remain `null` or empty. A title is not evidence of a benefit.

All newly imported Boosts default to `approved_for_ai_recommendation: false` until Terri supplies verified use cases and review decisions.

# Blissiree Brand Extraction

## Directly observed

The supplied logo is a wide white lockup containing a rounded organic `B` emblem and the Blissiree wordmark. The lettering is friendly, substantial, rounded, and sans-serif. The emblem uses soft lobed geometry rather than sharp corners. A restrained light-grey edge/shadow gives the white mark slight depth. Its transparent background and white fill require a dark or coloured placement surface and generous clear space.

The second supplied image is a Google Play Console License Testing screenshot, not a Blissiree homepage banner. It provides no valid Blissiree evidence and was excluded from the brand analysis.

## Not established by the supplied assets

No approved colour palette, brand typeface, website gradient, photography treatment, button style, card style, or motion system can be inferred from the logo alone.

## PWA implementation choices

The PWA preserves the logo without recolouring or distortion on a dark neutral surface. Rounded containers and controls echo the logo’s soft geometry. Subtle warm and grounded tonal lighting distinguish Emma and Ben while keeping one shared system. These atmospheric colours, serif display accents, gradients, shadows, and interaction treatments are implementation choices pending a correct homepage/banner reference; they are not asserted as official brand rules.

## Carry into the PWA

- White logo on a high-contrast surface
- Generous logo clear space
- Rounded, organic geometry
- Calm visual pacing and restrained dimensional depth
- Consistent lockup in desktop, mobile, splash, and loading contexts

## Do not copy literally

The wide wordmark should not be forced into a square app-icon crop. A separately approved square logo/icon asset is required for production store installation. Google Play Console navigation, colours, components, and typography are unrelated and must not be copied.

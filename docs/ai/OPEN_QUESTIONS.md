## Confirmed policy decisions

### Blissiree is non-medical

Confirmed by the project owner on 2026-08-10:

- Blissiree is not a medical or healthcare system.
- The system must not claim to hold or process medical records.
- It must not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or imply healthcare-provider affiliation.
- Safety detection exists only to constrain the conversation and direct a person to appropriate external emergency or professional support when necessary.
- Distress and recovery-state fields are conversational safety controls, not clinical assessments or medical data.
